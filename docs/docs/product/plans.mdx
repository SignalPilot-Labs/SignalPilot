# What each plan includes

SignalPilot Cloud has three plans: **Team**, **Scale** and **Enterprise**. Each is one
flat fee that includes a set of **allowances** (seats, covered data models, eval runs)
and a block of **credits** every month. Everything you use beyond what is included is
paid in credits at fixed rates. There are no feature tiers: every paid plan has every
feature.

The current fees, allowances, seat rates and credit rates are published in one place
and read from there everywhere they appear: the [pricing page](https://signalpilot.ai/pricing),
the plan cards and rate table under **Settings → Billing**, and the `GET /api/v1/billing/plans`
endpoint (public, no auth). This page explains how the plans work; the numbers live there.

## The one rule

**If your organization is on a paid plan, every feature is on.** Team, Scale
and Enterprise all qualify, as does a plan in its first 14 days past due.

There are no per-feature flags, no allowlists and no hidden features. Inside a
paid organization, org admins manage org-wide things (connections, connectors,
settings, API keys) and members use them; that is the only other distinction.

## Plans and terms

| | Team | Scale | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who it is for | one data team | several teams, or a large dbt project | a company-wide estate |
| How it is bought | self-serve | self-serve | one purchase order, custom terms |
| Included seats | published | published | 100, more by contract |
| Added seats | per seat-month, in credits | per seat-month, in credits | priced in the contract |
| Included covered models, eval runs, credits | published | published | published starting point, raised by contract |
| Managed (optional) | flat monthly add-on | flat monthly add-on | flat monthly add-on |

Billing is **annual**. A **three-month** term is available at a premium; the plan cards
show both. The flat fee is charged for the term up front; credit overage is added to a
monthly invoice. Enterprise allowances above the published starting point, custom credit
grants, SSO, SCIM, PrivateLink and customer-managed keys are agreed in the contract and
set on your entitlement by the SignalPilot team.

Included credits and allowances reset each calendar month and do not roll over,
whatever the billing term. Allowances are separate counters; they are never pooled
with credits.

## Credits and rates

1 credit = $0.01. Credits pay for per-use work and for anything beyond an
allowance. Rates are fixed; there are no brackets. The rate table lists a
credit price for:

| Item | Metered |
|---|---|
| Successful chat thread | per thread |
| Governed query | per query, everywhere (thread, Claude Code, notebook, schedule, eval run) |
| Covered model beyond the allowance | per model-month, metered daily |
| Eval run beyond the monthly allowance | per run (queries inside the run bill as queries) |
| Added seat beyond the allowance | per seat-month, metered daily; Enterprise seats are contracted instead |
| Model tokens on the SignalPilot key | provider list price times a published multiplier, per run |

Bring your own model key (BYOK) and the token line is 0.

A thread that fails, runs no queries, or cites no evidence is not charged.
Repeated questions in the same thread and threads flagged wrong by a reviewer
are returned. Work SignalPilot starts on its own behalf (readiness checks,
schema refreshes, dbt map compiles) never consumes credits.

When you have used your included credits, usage continues and the overage is
billed at $0.01 per credit on your next invoice. You are alerted at 80% and
100% of your included block. Nothing is ever blocked for running out of
credits.

## What a free organization sees

A free organization sees every page in the navigation. Where a paid
organization would see the working surface, a free organization sees a plan
prompt instead. Nothing is hidden, and nothing runs, so nothing is metered.

Requests from a free organization to a gated route answer `402` with the body
`{"error": "plan_required", "tier": "free"}`.

## What "not available in this deployment" means

Some features depend on infrastructure the deployment must be wired for:
evals need a runner image and an evidence store, and sandbox runtimes need
credentials for the sandbox provider. When that wiring is missing, the page
says so and the route answers `503` with
`{"error": "not_available_in_deployment", "capability": "evals"}`. This is
about the deployment, not your plan; on SignalPilot Cloud everything is
wired.

## Self-hosted deployments

A self-hosted gateway without a backend (`SP_BACKEND_URL` unset) treats its
single organization as unlimited: every feature is on and nothing is metered.
