<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your workspace">
    Sign up at [app.signalpilot.ai](https://app.signalpilot.ai). If a colleague invited you, accept the invitation and you land in their workspace with its connections and definitions already in place. Otherwise, name your workspace after your team. The free plan is enough to try everything on this page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect a database" illustration={<Warehouse />}>
    Open **Connections → Add connection**, choose your warehouse, and paste read-only credentials. SignalPilot supports Snowflake, BigQuery, Postgres, Redshift, Databricks, SQL Server, and more. The connection is tested before it is saved.

    No database handy? Turn on the [demo warehouse](/docs/product/demo-warehouse) and try everything against sample data first.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ask a question you can check" illustration={<Chat />}>
    Open **Chats**, pick the project, and ask something you already know the answer to, such as last month's order count. Read the answer, then open the evidence dropdown to see the SQL. If the number is right, you are set. If not, go to the next step.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Teach it your definitions" illustration={<Knowledge />}>
    Open the [knowledge base](/docs/product/knowledge-base) and write down the things your team knows but the schema does not: what counts as an active customer, which table is the source of truth for revenue, which fiscal calendar you use. Every later answer uses them automatically.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add SignalPilot to the AI tool you already use">
    You can stay in SignalPilot's chat, or bring the same governed access into your everyday tools.

    <PathCards>
      <PathCard title="Claude" description="Add SignalPilot as a connector in claude.ai." to="/docs/clients/claude" icon={<ClientMark client="claude" />} />
      <PathCard title="ChatGPT and OpenAI" description="ChatGPT connectors, Codex, and the Agents SDK." to="/docs/clients/chatgpt" icon={<ClientMark client="chatgpt" />} />
      <PathCard title="Claude Code" description="One command, plus skills for dbt and SQL work." to="/docs/clients/claude-code" icon={<ClientMark client="claude-code" />} />
      <PathCard title="Cursor and other tools" description="Any tool that supports MCP." to="/docs/clients/other-tools" icon={<ClientMark client="cursor" />} />
    </PathCards>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="note" title="Nothing is written to your database">
SignalPilot only ever reads. Statements that would change data are blocked before they run, results are capped, and every query is recorded in the [audit log](/docs/product/audit).
</Callout>
